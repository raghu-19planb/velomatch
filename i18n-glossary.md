# French (Québec) glossary for SelectVelo

Every French string on the site follows this list. Terms marked **OQLF** come from the Office québécois de la
langue française's published cycling vocabulary ("Vocabulaire du vélo") and its Grand dictionnaire
terminologique (vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca). Terms marked **VQ** follow how Vélo Québec writes.
Terms marked **usage** are what Québec shops and guides use where the OQLF has no entry: they are the ones a
native speaker should look at first.

The French text was written by an AI translator, not by a native speaker. It follows this glossary and
the OQLF's cycling vocabulary, but it has not had a human review. Anything without a French entry
falls back to English; it is never machine-translated on the fly.

## Style

- Address the reader as **vous**.
- Canadian format: 1 299 $ (space as thousands separator, comma decimal, $ after the number, non-breaking space
  before the $); money is shown as "$ CA" or "$ US".
- Punctuation follows the OQLF ("Espacement avant et après les signes de ponctuation et les symboles"): no space
  before ? ! ; and a non-breaking space before a colon and inside « guillemets ». The dictionaries are written with
  plain spaces; `i18n.js` applies the rule in one place (`I18N.fix`).
- Use the typographic apostrophe (’).
- Bike model names, brand names and product names are never translated.
- Units: cm, kg, km, mm stay as they are. Inches stay "po" only where a size name uses it ("roues de 20 po").
- Prefer plain, short sentences over word-for-word translations.
