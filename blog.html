VeloMatch

A smarter way to find a kid's first bike.

Fig. 006 — where this is headed

To everyone who's stood in a bike aisle guessing,

Buying a bike usually comes down to a guess: a size sticker on a rack, a salesperson pointing at whatever's in stock, or a color you liked in a catalog. Get it wrong and it's money spent on something that never quite fits.

We think fit should come from geometry, not guesswork — reach, stack, standover, all measured and matched to the rider, whoever that rider is.

VeloMatch is built to do that matching for every kind of rider and every kind of ride. Right now it's one working prototype: a first bike for a growing kid, because that's where a wrong guess costs the most and a growing body changes the fastest. The same approach — real measurements, scored against real bikes — is what we're building toward next, for every rider after this one.

It won't get everything right yet. But it will show its work — the wheel size, the standover, the years of growth room — so you're choosing a bike, not gambling on one.

This is where we're starting. Not where we're stopping.
